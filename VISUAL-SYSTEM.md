# Shapewear Store Visual System

Paste this at the top of every design prompt. Every page, every component, every round of iteration starts from this foundation.

## Mood
Dark architectural, cinematic, expensive. A high-end magazine spread, not a Shopify template.

## Colors
- Background: warm cream `#f7f1e8` — never pure white
- Text: near-black ink `#1a1714`
- Secondary text: warm gray `#8a7f72`
- Accent: muted gold `#c4a574` — sparingly, for labels, hover states, email section only
- Cards: white `#ffffff` with soft shadows that deepen on hover

## Typography
- Headlines: Cormorant Garamond, weight 500, tracking tight
- Body and UI: Inter, weight 300 to 500
- No gradients on text. No neon. No playful shapes.

## Photography
Real bodies, natural light, no filters, no stock poses. Photography is the only texture.

## Motion
Slow and confident. Fade-ups on scroll. Gentle hover lifts. One hero zoom that settles over twenty seconds.

## Buttons
Small, uppercase, letter-spaced, never rounded.

## Layout Rules
- Flat, deliberate, quiet
- One section at a time per iteration round
- Specific feedback each round, never "make it better"
- Mobile-first always
